The studio

Small team, sharp focus

Solara is an independent studio making games that are fun, creative, and a little unexpected. From skill-based arcade challenges on PC to lighthearted mobile adventures, we ship the kind of games we want to keep playing long after launch.

Every project starts with one question: does the core loop feel good in the first ten seconds? If it doesn't, it doesn't ship.
